# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use several coding assistants on macOS and need a glanceable view of usage limits and live agent activity without interrupting their work.

## Product Purpose

Codenotch keeps assistant capacity and activity visible at a screen edge. Success means a user can tell which assistant has room, which is working, and which needs attention without opening each provider.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and native.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard chrome, decorative cards, ornamental motion, and controls that feel foreign to macOS. The notch should not compete with the work happening beside it.

## Design Principles

- Make status readable at a glance.
- Preserve the compact hardware-like silhouette.
- Use familiar macOS controls in settings.
- Keep provider data honest about freshness and fidelity.
- Let customization support recognition without weakening semantic warnings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor reduced-motion preferences, expose meaningful labels for icon-only controls, preserve keyboard operation, and maintain readable contrast against the black notch surface.
